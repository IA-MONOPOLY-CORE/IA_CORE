# Roadmap 4.x Macro-Mission 04.1

## DOOL permanent generational lineage

## Contract

`DOOL_IS_A_PERMANENT_CORE_NODE_FAMILY`

`GENERATION_ZERO_IS_ORIGIN_NOT_SUNSET`

`DEVELOPMENT_ORIGIN_IS_PROVENANCE_NOT_LIFETIME`

`FIELD_OPERATION_EXTENDS_DOOL_AND_DOES_NOT_REPLACE_IT`

`DOOL_LEARNS_FROM_OWNER_SYSTEM_AGENTS_AND_ENTERPRISES`

`DOOL_PRESERVES_GENERATIONAL_LINEAGE`

`DOOL_PRODUCES_CANDIDATES_GOKV_GOVERNS_OCI_INHERITS`

`EACH_STAGE_BUILDS_THE_SYSTEM_AND_IMPROVES_ITS_NEXT_BUILDER`

DOOL is the permanent operational-learning family for the ecosystem. It
records how the system was built, what was learned from the Owner and later
what is learned in controlled operation. The development origin is lineage and
provenance; it is not an expiry date and it is not a disposable bootstrap.

## Generation contract

| Node space | Allowed source | 04.1 state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `G0_DEVELOPMENT_ORIGIN` | Repository evidence and explicit Owner direction | Materialized in the inert graph |
| `G1_BETA_OPERATIONAL_LEARNING` | Controlled beta evidence with consent and isolation | Contracted placeholder |
| `G2_FIELD_OPERATIONAL_LEARNING` | Sanitized, auditable field outcomes | Contracted placeholder |
| `GN_CONTINUOUS_EVOLUTION` | Repeated evidence of method improvement | Contracted placeholder |

Only G0 contains real nodes now. G1, G2 and Gn contain no invented results,
metrics, enterprise memories, agent performance or field claims. A future
generation must arrive through an explicit contract and independent evidence;
it cannot overwrite or silently reinterpret history.

## What DOOL may contain

G0 may contain distilled operational units such as explicit decisions,
detected problems, verified repairs, limits of validity, failures of process,
recovery strategies, test patterns and improvements to the Method Santi. Each
unit needs provenance, evidence, scope, privacy and lifecycle. It must not be a
raw transcript, private reasoning record, conversation dump, password, token,
credential, tenant secret or model-weight update.

## Relation to GOKV and OCI

GOKV stores and governs knowledge records and evidence. DOOL supplies the
development-origin lineage and learning context. OCI selects only compatible,
authorized and evidence-backed capability for a specific development mission.
DOOL does not grant permission and OCI does not promote DOOL automatically.

The flow remains:

```text
development evidence -> distilled DOOL unit -> GOKV lifecycle -> explicit OCI selection
```

Every stage can improve the next builder, but neither success nor failure
becomes globally inheritable without validation, privacy filtering and a
current-contract check.

## Lifetime and rollback

DOOL remains available when field operation begins. A later correction creates
lineage or supersession evidence; it does not delete the origin. Rollback is a
reviewable document/record decision and cannot reactivate revoked access,
restore secrets or cross a tenant boundary.
