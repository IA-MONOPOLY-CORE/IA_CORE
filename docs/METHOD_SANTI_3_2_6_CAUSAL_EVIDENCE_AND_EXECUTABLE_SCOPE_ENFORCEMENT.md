# Method Santi 3.2.6 - Causal Evidence and Executable Scope Enforcement

This is an additive method evolution after 3.2.5. It is a method principle,
not product authority, business authority or runtime.

## Principles

* `NO_EVIDENCE_FROM_THE_FUTURE`: a run must occur before canonical evidence
  finalization and after its validation basis commit.
* `NO_DECLARED_CONTROL_WITHOUT_EXECUTABLE_ENFORCEMENT`: policy vocabulary is
  not a control until a gate applies it to live state.
* `NO_SELF_DECLARED_PROTECTED_DIFF`: protected results are computed from Git.
* `NO_POST_FINALIZATION_EVENT_INSIDE_PRE_FINALIZATION_EVIDENCE`: later events
  belong to receipts and the postpublish envelope.
* `CI_STEP_IS_NOT_REQUIRED_CHECK`: status identity is a real job contract.
* `REMOTE_ENFORCEMENT_MUST_HAVE_AN_EVOLVABLE_STATE_MODEL`: local proof,
  verified external proof and stale external proof are distinct states.
* `DEVELOPMENTAL_FAILURES_MAY_FEED_BOUNDED_VERO_AND_FIRE_PROFILES`: learning
  may produce documentary developmental exercises without creating runtime.
* `OPERATOR_ACCEPTANCE_REMAINS_REQUIRED_BEFORE_NEXT_FAMILY`: P3 remains
  selected but not started.

## Non-duplication decision

3.2.5 establishes executable closure authority, canonical evidence and
renderer-owned completion. 3.2.6 adds the missing causal boundary: Git-derived
scope, temporal station order, receipt separation, evolvable remote state and
the bounded VERO/FIRE feedback loop. It therefore does not duplicate 3.2.5;
it closes the audited causality and scope gap.
