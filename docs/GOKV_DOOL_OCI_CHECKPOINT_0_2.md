## What Is Institutionalized

DOOL is now an explicit origin model for knowledge produced during IA_CORE gestation. OCI is a development-time selection/compilation mechanism that delivers relevant operational capability without copying the complete vault. Provenance and lineage can represent future field confirmation, refinement, limitation, contradiction, and supersession. Promotion is assessed objectively but remains non-automatic.
