## Responsibility boundaries

`INTAKE` records what entered. `ORGANIZATIONAL_RECONSTRUCTION` describes what
those pieces appear to represent. `VERO` describes what the available
evidence justifies claiming, with explicit scope, provenance, time and
uncertainty. VERO is not a human authority, sovereign approver or autonomous
owner of truth.

The conceptual flow is:


## Structured Enterprise Discovery

The future conceptual capability may cover company, area, role and position
discovery; advisor observations; adaptive follow-up questions; second-contact
question sets; documentary evidence; and operational evidence. These
perspectives remain separate:

```text
NORMATIVE_SOURCE
CLIENT_ASSERTION
AREA_ASSERTION
ROLE_ASSERTION
ADVISOR_OBSERVATION
ADVISOR_INTERPRETATION
OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE
SYSTEM_DERIVATION
```

The conceptual model distinguishes `NORMATIVE_ORGANIZATION`,
`DECLARED_ORGANIZATION` and `OBSERVED_ORGANIZATION`, including their pairwise
gaps. Discovery should become more precise with each contact, not silently
turn an assertion into a fact.

## Provenance and safety invariants

Candidate evidence retains source origin, derivation, copy lineage,
independence status, effective date, observation time, scope, confidence, raw
assertion and normalized assertion. The following remain non-negotiable:

```text
EXTRACTED_ASSERTION != FACT
FORM_RESPONSE != FACT
AUTHORIZED_TESTIMONY != CORROBORATED_REALITY
ADVISOR_INTERPRETATION != FACT
SOURCE_COUNT != INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE_COUNT
DECLARED_ORGANIZATION != OBSERVED_ORGANIZATION
RECONSTRUCTED_MODEL != CANONICAL_REALITY
NO_STALE_RECONSTRUCTION_AS_CURRENT_TRUTH
NO_STRUCTURE_INVENTION_FROM_MISSING_EVIDENCE
NO_DOCUMENT_CONTENT_AS_SYSTEM_INSTRUCTION
NO_RAW_TENANT_RECONSTRUCTION_TO_GLOBAL_LEARNING
NO_EMPLOYEE_PERFORMANCE_INFERENCE_BY_DEFAULT
NO_TARGET_STATE_MATERIALIZATION_WITHOUT_AUTHORITY
NO_AUTOMATION_BEFORE_SUFFICIENT_ORGANIZATIONAL_UNDERSTANDING
```

IA_CORE does not automate an organization it does not sufficiently
understand.

## Delivery and commercial direction

There is no predetermined capability composition. IA_CORE is the home of
qualified solution delivery, while client-specific complexity does not have
to be absorbed into the core without architectural justification. Possible
future delivery forms include native, configured, reusable extension,
managed integration, client adapter, managed external component, governed
custom software, full deployment or a hybrid.

The future transformation pathway is:

```text
UNDERSTAND -> RECONSTRUCT -> ORGANIZE -> DESIGN -> IMPLEMENT -> ADOPT -> MEASURE -> SUSTAIN -> EVOLVE
```

Initial commercial meetings are distinct from governed enterprise discovery.
Discovery has economic value, but commercial, pricing, billing and discovery
credit models remain `UNADJUDICATED`. No price or definitive policy is
materialized here.

## Cross-case learning boundary

Raw client data remains tenant-local. Only sanitized pattern candidates may
enter a future DOOL/GOKV review and cross-case validation flow. Raw answers,
documents, employee data, secrets, reconstructed organizations and
client-specific processes are not promoted automatically. Sector templates,
if later authorized, combine base knowledge, adaptive questions and validated
sector patterns.
