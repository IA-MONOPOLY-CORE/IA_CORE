## Explicit non-claims

The direction does not prove operational learning, beta or field results,
exceptional performance, tenant ownership, external exposure, recovery,
provider availability, or production security. `OWNER_DIRECTED` records intent;
it is not a promotion decision. No claim is promoted merely because it appears
in this document or in the G0 graph.

## Precedence

Security and privacy hard contracts, the current mission constraints and the
current canonical architecture continue to outrank guidance. A conflict is
excluded and recorded; it is never silently resolved by OCI. P1-A remains
`SELECTED_NOT_STARTED` and `TEMPORARILY_PAUSED_BEHIND_COGNITIVE_KERNEL_RECONCILIATION`.

training path, company, agent hierarchy, broker, bank, trading operation or
