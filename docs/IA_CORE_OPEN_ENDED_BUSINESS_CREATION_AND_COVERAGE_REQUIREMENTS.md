# IA_CORE - Open-Ended Business Creation and Coverage Requirements

## Gate

`ROADMAP_3_0_A_N1_BUSINESS_CREATION_REQUIREMENTS_PASSED`

## Strategic requirement

IA_CORE must be a platform for composing businesses, not a closed list of
businesses, occupations or traditional job categories. The current library of
30 areas, 200 niches and 106 professional profiles is **initial critical mass**.
It is not a maximum, quota or artificial ceiling.

The future architecture must be able to represent physical, digital and hybrid
businesses; SaaS; services; retail; gastronomy; independent professionals;
creators; marketplaces; platforms; automated businesses; AI-native businesses;
agent-based businesses; and future forms that do not fit today's taxonomy.

## Coverage before expansion

Expansion is justified only after a coverage audit. The future audit must answer:

1. What business surface is covered today?
2. Which business types can be composed completely?
3. Which are only partial?
4. Which business models are missing?
5. Which gaps are professional gaps versus combinations of existing pieces?
6. Which gaps need profiles, roles, specializations, areas, niches or team templates?
7. Which gaps are future and should remain deferred?

The result must separate `WHAT_EXISTS`, `WHAT_IS_READY`, `WHAT_IS_PARTIAL`,
`WHAT_IS_MISSING`, `WHAT_CAN_BE_COMPOSED` and `WHAT_REQUIRES_EXPANSION`.

## Extensible taxonomy

Areas, niches, profiles, roles and specializations remain reusable catalog
entities, but the composition contract must accept new business types and
capability combinations without requiring a fixed universal enumeration. A new
category is not automatically justified by a missing label. The preferred path is:

`MASS_CRITICAL_INITIAL_LIBRARY -> COVERAGE_AUDIT -> GAP_DETECTION ->
JUSTIFIED_EXPANSION -> TESTS -> FIELD_EVIDENCE -> EVOLUTION`.

This is extensibility by contract, not uncontrolled catalog growth.

## Capability composition

The future composition layer should derive requirements from:

`BUSINESS_REQUIREMENTS -> REQUIRED_CAPABILITIES -> REQUIRED_ROLES ->
AGENT_BLUEPRINTS -> TEAMS -> MODEL/MEMORY POLICIES -> ORGANIZATION`.

Business composition must not infer readiness merely because a matching area,
niche or profile label exists. It must trace capabilities, limitations,
responsibilities, permissions, memory, model policy, hierarchy and team
compatibility.

## Status

This document is a `FUTURE_STRATEGIC_DIRECTION` and `FUTURE_REQUIREMENT`.
No business composition layer, coverage engine, taxonomy expansion or new
catalog entry is implemented by Roadmap 3.0.A.
