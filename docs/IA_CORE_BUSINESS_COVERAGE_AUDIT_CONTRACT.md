# IA_CORE - Future Business Coverage Audit Contract

## Gate

`ROADMAP_3_0_A_N6_COVERAGE_AUDIT_CONTRACT_PASSED`

## Purpose

This is the future contract for an extreme business coverage audit. It must
distinguish what exists from what is ready, partial, missing, composable and
actually requires expansion. Roadmap 3.0.A defines the contract but does not
execute the complete audit.

## Required matrix dimensions

| Dimension | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| `BUSINESS_TYPE` | physical, digital, hybrid, SaaS, service, retail, gastronomy, independent, creator, marketplace, platform, automated, AI-native and future class |
| `BUSINESS_MODEL` | revenue/operating pattern and assumptions, without treating current categories as exhaustive |
| `BUSINESS_SCALE` | solo, micro, small, medium, enterprise and relevant future scale |
| `REQUIRED_CAPABILITIES` | business functions and quality/limit criteria |
| `REQUIRED_ROLES` | professional roles and responsibility boundaries |
| `AGENT_BLUEPRINTS` | reusable professional DNA and provenance |
| `READY/PARTIAL/MISSING` | status with evidence and blockers |
| `TEAM_TEMPLATE` | compatible team structure and coordination requirements |
| `MEMORY_REQUIREMENTS` | agent/team/business memory, sharing, retention and isolation |
| `MODEL_REQUIREMENTS` | workload, reasoning, privacy, latency and provider policy |
| `IDEAL_HIERARCHY` | full organization needed by the business |
| `MINIMUM_HIERARCHY` | smallest safe/useful organization |
| `HARDWARE_FITTED_HIERARCHY` | currently runnable organization and concurrency schedule |
| `READINESS` | explicit readiness decision, provenance, unknowns and human boundary |

## Audit output

Every row must answer:

`WHAT_EXISTS` / `WHAT_IS_READY` / `WHAT_IS_PARTIAL` / `WHAT_IS_MISSING` /
`WHAT_CAN_BE_COMPOSED` / `WHAT_REQUIRES_EXPANSION`.

Gaps must be classified as a new combination of existing pieces, a missing
professional profile, missing role or specialization, missing area or niche,
missing team template, missing model/memory policy, a security/permission gap,
or a future concept that should remain deferred.

## Evidence and non-inference rules

- Catalog presence is not business readiness.
- A profile is not an agent blueprint until capability, limits, provenance,
  model, memory, permission and team compatibility are evidenced.
- A team template is not a running team.
- A model policy is not a model installation or provider availability proof.
- A memory field is not logical isolation proof.
- Unknown deployment, security, hardware or provider facts remain unknown.
- Expansion requires evidence and tests; decorative taxonomy growth is invalid.

## Future status

`FUTURE_REQUIREMENT`, `FUTURE_STRATEGIC_DIRECTION`. No complete coverage audit,
business composition layer, gap engine or taxonomy expansion is implemented by
Roadmap 3.0.A.
