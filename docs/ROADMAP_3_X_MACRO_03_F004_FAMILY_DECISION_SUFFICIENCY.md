## Decision-sufficiency vocabulary

| Field | Values used here | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Destination class | `KEEP_CANONICAL`, `MIGRATE_TO_CANONICAL`, `REPLACE_WITH_SUCCESSOR`, `INTERNAL_ONLY`, `REMOVE`, `UNKNOWN` | What the route is authorized to become; all current routes remain `UNKNOWN`. |
| Decision sufficiency | `EVIDENCE_SUFFICIENT`, `SPECIFICATION_READY`, `REMEDIATION_READY`, `EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`, `DIRECTION_DECISION_REQUIRED`, `DEFERRED_BY_PHASE`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` | Whether the next decision can be made from current evidence. |
| Specification readiness | `SPECIFICATION_READY` | A bounded future specification can state required proof without granting authority. |
| Remediation readiness | `NOT_READY_REQUIRES_DIRECTION_OR_EVIDENCE` | No route may be changed until authority, compatibility and required evidence exist. |

`SPECIFICATION_READY` never means implementation-ready. It means that the
missing contract, evidence producer, rollback boundary and stop condition can
be specified without changing product semantics.
