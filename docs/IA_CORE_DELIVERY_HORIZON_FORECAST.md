## Main forecast risks

- Direction may accept evidence with limits but defer route authority;
- external deployment or provider facts may remain unavailable;
- a selected family may reveal a second true hard frontier;
- product persistence and recovery ownership may require a separate contract;
- backend maturity may precede UI/UX completion by more than one bounded
  release cycle.

The forecast must be updated whenever a frontier is dissolved, a new external
fact is observed, a Direction decision is recorded or a future scope changes.
