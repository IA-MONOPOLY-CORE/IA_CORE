# IA_CORE Enterprise Foundry and Agent Lineage

## State and boundary

`FUTURE_CONCEPT_PRESERVED_NOT_SCHEDULED_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`

This document preserves a future direction for IA_CORE. It is a strategic
record only. It does not activate a feature, create an endpoint, define a
production schema, create a company or agent, grant permission, or change the
Roadmap 4.x order. It is outside the active scope of Macro-Mission 04.

The concept does not authorize implementation, public exposure, economic
autonomy, hardware purchases, or a promise of profitability. Any future use
requires separate research, product contracts, simulation, security, privacy,
legal, execution, and end-to-end missions with explicit human acceptance.

## Constitutional authority

Santi is the maximum authority of IA_CORE. IA_CORE Global belongs to and
answers to the Owner. An agent never obtains root or global access through
performance. Important, delicate, legal, financial, or irreversible decisions
remain under the Owner's final authority or an explicitly pre-authorized
policy. Autonomy removes repetitive operational work; it does not remove
governance or responsibility.

The following invariants are part of the preserved direction:

- `OWNER_FINAL_AUTHORITY`
- `SUCCESS_NEVER_GRANTS_ROOT`
- `ENTERPRISE_LOCAL_REALITY_BOUNDARY`
- `PROMOTION_IS_EVIDENCE_BOUND_AND_REVERSIBLE`
- `FOUNDING_IS_A_GATED_CAPABILITY`
- `PROPOSAL_IS_NOT_APPROVAL`
- `APPROVAL_IS_NOT_EXECUTION`
- `CHILD_NODES_INHERIT_MATURITY_NOT_TENANT_SECRETS`
- `GLOBAL_LEARNING_FLOWS_UPWARD_AUTHORITY_DOES_NOT`
- `REPLICATION_IS_QUOTA_BOUND_AND_REVERSIBLE`

Each enterprise is the visible operating world for its agents. An agent must
not learn the existence, data, secrets, or strategy of another enterprise
unless an explicit contract provides sanitized information. Earned authority
is local, bounded, revocable, and auditable.

## Universal creation selector

The future product concept uses a universal `+` selector. It may eventually
offer these conceptual choices:

- `EMPRESA_COMPLETA`
- `SECTOR_O_UNIDAD_ESPECIFICA`
- `PERSONALIZADO`

The architectural distinction is retained as two independent dimensions:

- `creation_scope`: complete enterprise, or a sector, unit, or team inside an
  existing enterprise;
- `creation_mode`: guided, or custom.

Those dimensions describe four future combinations: complete enterprise
guided/custom and specific unit guided/custom. The first result is always a
Draft. The selector cannot deploy, activate, connect integrations, consume
real capital, grant permissions, create credentials, or execute an external
task. A proposal is not approval, and approval is not execution.

## Agent maturity and promotion

The future conceptual levels are:

`candidate` -> `specialist` -> `senior` -> `leader` -> `director` ->
`founder candidate` -> `founder agent`

These names are not a production schema and may change. Promotion is
multidimensional: performance, production, reliability, compliance, resource
efficiency, decision quality, stability, learning, team contribution,
behavior under pressure, resistance to metric manipulation, and respect for
limits and authority. Profit, volume, or one isolated metric is insufficient.

The future promotion decision must be evidence-bound, reversible, scoped, and
auditable. A successful result can support a review; it cannot grant root,
global access, or self-approval.

## Agent Capability Passport

`AGENT_CAPABILITY_PASSPORT` is a future, sanitized, verifiable and portable
representation of an agent's maturity. It may contain demonstrated
competencies, specializations, aggregated performance evidence, decision
quality, mastered tools, validated general patterns, learned errors,
compliance history, reliability, lineage, reached authority level, and the
conditions under which performance was valid.

It must never contain passwords, tokens, secrets, credentials, raw tenant
memory, personal data, customer data, private commercial strategy, sensitive
internal prompts, secret configuration, unauthorized source-enterprise
information, inherited access, or references that can recover secrets.

## Founding Lease

`FOUNDING_LEASE` is a future capability, not an active lease. It is signed,
limited, revocable, temporary, budgeted, auditable, mission-specific, and
unable to grant global access.

A founder candidate may propose an enterprise and purpose, design a team,
request resources, define objectives and metrics, prepare a sandbox, and
propose a growth strategy. It may not approve its own founding, activate
itself, assign capital, issue credentials, alter its evaluation, hide logs,
transfer money, inherit secrets, create unlimited descendants, remove
controls, or change the global constitution.

## Logical enterprise and real legal entity

The future model distinguishes four layers:

1. a logical enterprise inside IA_CORE;
2. an internal business unit;
3. an isolated tenant or subtenant;
4. a real legal entity.

Logical enterprise creation may eventually be automated under its own
contract. A legal person, bank account, broker account, tax obligation, or
other external commitment depends on applicable law, providers, and human
authorization. The model does not assume that every internal node needs a
separate legal company.

## Treasury and distribution

`OWNER_DISTRIBUTION_POLICY` is a future policy concept. A business may produce
economic flow and the Owner may define a periodic distribution policy, but a
distribution depends on actually settled funds, liquidity, reserves,
obligations, taxes, drawdown, risk, and authorized policy. A target is a
conditional target, never a guarantee, and it must never force increased risk
to reach a number.

Any future operation requires official adapters, a secrets vault, bounded
capabilities, pre-authorized destinations, limits, traceability, revocation,
and extra approval where risk demands it. A general agent must never receive
plaintext passwords or directly control the vault. This document does not
connect banks, brokers, payment systems, or external execution.

## Controlled growth

Future contracts may consider maximum global nodes, nodes per enterprise,
lineage depth, descendants per founder, capital at risk, compute and data
budgets, correlation limits, creation cooldown, market coverage, compatibility,
marginal cost, hardware capacity, expiry, quarantine, retirement, and a kill
switch. No numeric limit is fixed here. Every growth capability must be
quota-bound, reversible, and subject to Owner or policy authority.

## Reusable kernel and enterprise skins

The future architecture preserves this direction:

`GLOBAL_CAPABILITY_KERNEL` -> `DOMAIN_ADAPTER` -> `ENTERPRISE_POLICY` ->
`LOCAL_EXECUTION`

Trading may be a demanding test field for risk, allocation, challenger
systems, promotion, hierarchy, treasury, audit, observability, simulation,
limits, execution, and resilience. Success in Trading alone cannot promote a
capability globally.

The conceptual lifecycle is:

- `LOCAL_ONLY`
- `REUSABLE_CANDIDATE`
- `CROSS_DOMAIN_VALIDATED`
- `GLOBAL_PRIMITIVE`

Promotion requires evidence of compatibility in another context. This future
classification must integrate with or map to the existing GOKV mechanism when
its design phase arrives; it does not create a second knowledge vault or
duplicate GOKV now.

## Hardware-aware growth

IA_CORE should scale from accessible hardware toward larger infrastructure
without depending on one future purchase. Model, agent, and workload
placement should remain hardware-aware. A local station, multiple nodes,
cloud, or future DGX/HGX-class infrastructure are possible destinations, not
commitments. A hardware purchase requires evidence of load, return, privacy,
latency, and cost. This record authorizes no purchase, integration, or final
hardware selection.
