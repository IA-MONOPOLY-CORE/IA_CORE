# METHOD_SANTI_3_2_5_EXECUTABLE_CLOSURE_AUTHORITY_ENGINEERING

This is an additive evolution of Method Santi 3.2.4. The 3.2.4 document remains
unchanged and remains the historical canonical-evidence method.

## Closure Authority Separation

```text
AGENT_PROPOSES_CLOSURE
EVIDENCE_SUPPORTS_CLOSURE
EXECUTABLE_GATE_ADJUDICATES_CLOSURE
OPERATOR_ACCEPTS_OR_REJECTS_NEXT_STEP
```

`AGENT_NARRATIVE_HAS_ZERO_CLOSURE_AUTHORITY` and
`EXECUTABLE_CLOSURE_GATE_IS_THE_ONLY_CLOSURE_AUTHORITY` are constitutional
constraints. The report is not an adjudicator.

## Evidence before prose

Structured evidence is complete before the final report is rendered. The report
is generated from the validated evidence bytes and is never manually reconstructed
afterwards. `REPORT_COMPLETENESS_GATE: PASS` is authoritative only when emitted by
the gate with a valid evidence and report receipt.

## Validation-basis invalidation

Every path changed after Level B is classified by the manifest as exactly one of
`DOCUMENTARY_ONLY`, `EVIDENCE_ONLY`, `EXECUTABLE`, `TEST_INFRASTRUCTURE`,
`CONFIGURATION`, or `PRODUCT`. Executable, test-infrastructure, configuration, and
product changes invalidate the basis and require the affected validation again,
including Level B when collection, execution, or global results may change.
Documentary and evidence changes require post-evidence and prelock gates and may
not alter validated claims.

## Regression prevention

```text
NO_SELF_DECLARED_PASS
NO_REPORT_ONLY_CLOSURE
NO_EVIDENCE_BY_OMISSION
NO_FINAL_HEAD_WITH_UNVALIDATED_EXECUTABLE_DIFF
NO_SECOND_REQUEST_FOR_MANDATORY_INFORMATION
CLOSURE_GATE_APPLIES_TO_ALL_FUTURE_GOVERNED_MISSIONS
```

## Frontier

```text
METHOD_UPDATE_IS_NOT_PRODUCT_CAPABILITY
METHOD_UPDATE_IS_NOT_RUNTIME
METHOD_UPDATE_IS_NOT_AUTHORITY_SOURCE_FOR_BUSINESS_ACTIONS
METHOD_UPDATE_IS_NOT_GOKV_PROMOTION
```

The gate adjudicates engineering closure only. It does not grant business,
security, runtime, tenant, integration, provider, or execution permission.
